# Impact Start: product walkthrough

*An independent concept prototype for the Program Launch Lead / Director application at High Impact
Professionals. Not an official HIP product.*

**Try it:** open the deployed site and choose **View a sample profile** for a 2-minute tour with a
fictional participant, or **Discover your impact pathways** for the full 10–15 minute experience.

## The idea

HIP wants a two-hour introductory experience for professionals new to high-impact careers. Impact Start
is its first fifteen minutes: an interactive entry point that helps someone see their experience
differently, then hands them one concrete experiment and the next step in HIP's learning journey.

## The journey in seven moments

1. **Invitation.** "What could your experience make possible?" One clear action and an honest note on
   how answers are used.
2. **Swipe Discovery.** Eight quick statements ("People rely on me to make complex organisations run
   better"). Swipe, tap or use arrow keys. A short tutorial can be skipped. Back and pause work
   everywhere. A live map on the side shows themes forming from the answers.
3. **Career Crossroads.** Three realistic dilemmas: a pay-cut offer, an overlooked transferable skill,
   and limited time. Choices get neutral reflections, never "Great choice!".
4. **The Living Impact Map.** Capabilities, motivations and pathway hypotheses, connected by lines that
   mean something: solid lines link themes from the same answer; dashed lines are hypotheses. Select
   anything to see its evidence; remove anything that's wrong.
5. **The Reveal.** Seven slides: your story (including your own words), hidden professional capital,
   what drives you, the map, possibilities, the important unknowns, and one seven-day experiment.
6. **Pathways.** Three differentiated hypotheses (adjacent, stretch, without changing jobs) with need,
   mechanism, assumptions, constraints, background reading and a cheap test. Compare them in categories,
   save one, choose one to investigate, or set one aside and get a replacement.
7. **Impact Passport and learning.** A printable record and a privacy-safe summary to share, followed
   by a first lesson on judging whether a path is high-impact, linking only to HIP's own resources.

## Why this is a launch-ready shape, not just a demo

* **Measurable from day one.** 19 analytics events cover the funnel from landing to completed lesson,
  each stamped with question-bank, engine, library and prompt versions so iterations stay comparable.
  No answer content is ever sent.
* **Safe to iterate.** Questions, pathways and sources are data files. A dev-only evaluation console
  shows how any fictional profile is read, ranked and validated.
* **Honest by construction.** The validator rejects unsupported claims, invented figures and unknown
  sources, and the UI always says how results were produced.
* **Works without AI.** The rules engine runs on the server and in the browser; AI-assisted wording is
  optional and server-side only.

## What I would test first with real participants

Can people say what the product is for after the landing page? Do they recognise themselves in the
reveal? Can they explain why a pathway was suggested? Do they choose an experiment, and do they run it?
None of these outcomes has been tested yet.
